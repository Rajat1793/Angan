# Privacy Policy — Angan

Angan ("we", "us", "the app") is a community-management app for apartment
societies, operated by Rajat. This policy explains
what we collect, why, and your choices.

## Information we collect

- **Account information** — name, email address, and phone number you provide (or
  that your society admin provides) to create and use your account.
- **Society & unit data** — your flat/tower, role (resident, guard, admin), and
  the society you belong to.
- **Visitor & gate data** — visitor names, phone numbers, purpose, vehicle
  details, and **photos** captured at the gate for security records.
- **Community & activity data** — posts, comments, poll votes, tickets, bookings,
  and notices you create or interact with.
- **Payment data** — maintenance-dues payments are processed by our payment
  provider, **Razorpay**. We do **not** collect or store your card/bank details;
  we store only the payment amount, status, and transaction reference.
- **Device & notification data** — a push-notification token to deliver alerts.

## How we use your information

- To operate core features: visitor approvals, notices, amenities, payments,
  directory, and notifications.
- To secure the premises (visitor logs and gate records).
- To send you relevant alerts (approvals, dues, community activity).
- To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the app.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## Camera & permissions

- **Camera** — used to capture visitor photos and scan pre-approval QR passes.
- **Notifications** — used to deliver gate approvals and society alerts.
- **Internet** — used to sync with our secure backend.

Angan does **not** access your microphone, contacts, or location.

## Data sharing

We share data only as needed to run the service:
- With **Supabase** (our backend/hosting provider) to store and sync your data.
- With **Razorpay** to process payments.
- With other members of **your society**, per your role (e.g. a resident's post is
  visible to the community; a visitor record is visible to guards/admins).
- When required by law.

## Data security & retention

- Data is encrypted **in transit** (HTTPS/TLS).
- Access is restricted per society and per role (row-level security).
- We retain data while your account/society is active and as required for
  security and legal purposes.

## Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or **deletion** of your personal data
by contacting us at **rajat.jas4321@gmail.com**. You can also ask your society
admin to remove your account.

## Children

Angan is intended for adult residents and staff and is not directed at children.
